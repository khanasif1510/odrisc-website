# ODRISC Website Glossary

This glossary defines approved terminology and abbreviations used throughout the website and repository.

| Term | Definition | Audience Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ODRISC | Maternal and fetal intelligence platform | Use consistently |
| Longitudinal intelligence | Understanding how maternal and fetal information changes across pregnancy over time | Explain in plain language when patient-facing |
| Clinical decision support | Information that assists clinician judgment without replacing it | Avoid implying autonomous diagnosis |
| Maternal-fetal intelligence | Synchronized interpretation of maternal and fetal data across pregnancy | Core positioning term |
| GDM | Gestational diabetes mellitus | Write in full on first use |
| GWG | Gestational weight gain | Write in full on first use |
| EFW | Estimated fetal weight | Clinical audience |
| FGR | Fetal growth restriction | Clinical audience |
| MFM | Maternal-fetal medicine | Clinical audience |
| Patient journey | The audience-specific ODRISC website experience for women planning pregnancy, pregnant women, and relevant supporters | Use for website architecture |
| Provider journey | The audience-specific ODRISC website experience for clinicians, healthcare teams, hospitals, and institutional care partners | Use for website architecture |
| Healthcare provider | A qualified professional or organization involved in healthcare delivery | Preferred general Provider term pending final gateway wording |
| High-risk pregnancy | A pregnancy requiring increased clinical attention based on individual maternal, fetal, or pregnancy-related factors | Do not use as a diagnosis without appropriate context |
| Preconception | The period before pregnancy during which health, risk, and readiness may be assessed or supported | Patient and Provider audiences |
| Assisted reproduction | Clinical methods used to support conception, including IVF and related pathways | Define when first used in Patient content |
| Clinical workflow | The sequence of professional activities through which care, review, documentation, or monitoring is delivered | Provider audience |
| Maternal and fetal intelligence platform | A connected system that organizes and interprets maternal, fetal, clinical, and patient-reported information across pregnancy | Preferred description of ODRISC |
| Longitudinal intelligence | Structured understanding derived from information collected and compared across time | Core ODRISC product concept |
| Maternal intelligence | Longitudinal understanding of maternal risk, health, symptoms, weight, laboratory information, and pregnancy progression | Do not imply autonomous diagnosis |
| Fetal growth intelligence | Longitudinal understanding of fetal biometry, estimated fetal weight, growth trajectory, velocity, percentiles, and related clinical context | Provider-focused capability |
| Clinical decision support | Information, calculations, prompts, or summaries intended to assist professional judgment without replacing it | Use with explicit limitations |
| Explainable intelligence | A product output that communicates the data, rules, patterns, or rationale underlying an observation | Preferred over black-box AI |
| Growth velocity | The rate at which a fetal measurement or estimated fetal weight changes over time | Requires appropriately spaced measurements |
| Centile crossing | Movement across fetal-growth percentile bands over time | Must be interpreted clinically |
| Gestational-age anchor | The approved pregnancy-dating reference used to maintain a consistent gestational timeline | May use IVF, early ultrasound, or other approved sources |
| Patient-reported information | Information entered or communicated by the patient, including symptoms, measurements, adherence, or observations | Must be distinguished from clinician-verified information |
| Product status | The approved maturity classification of a capability, such as available, pilot, in development, or planned | Required before public communication |
| Cohort review | Provider or institutional review of information across a defined group of patients | Provider and enterprise capability |
