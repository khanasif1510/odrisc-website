# ODRISC Website Glossary

This glossary defines approved terminology and abbreviations used throughout the website and repository.

| Term                                     | Definition                                                                                                                                                | Audience Notes                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ODRISC                                   | Maternal and fetal intelligence platform                                                                                                                  | Use consistently                                                           |
| Longitudinal intelligence                | Understanding how maternal and fetal information changes across pregnancy over time                                                                       | Explain in plain language when patient-facing                              |
| Clinical decision support                | Information that assists clinician judgment without replacing it                                                                                          | Avoid implying autonomous diagnosis                                        |
| Maternal-fetal intelligence              | Synchronized interpretation of maternal and fetal data across pregnancy                                                                                   | Core positioning term                                                      |
| GDM                                      | Gestational diabetes mellitus                                                                                                                             | Write in full on first use                                                 |
| GWG                                      | Gestational weight gain                                                                                                                                   | Write in full on first use                                                 |
| EFW                                      | Estimated fetal weight                                                                                                                                    | Clinical audience                                                          |
| FGR                                      | Fetal growth restriction                                                                                                                                  | Clinical audience                                                          |
| MFM                                      | Maternal-fetal medicine                                                                                                                                   | Clinical audience                                                          |
| Patient journey                          | The audience-specific ODRISC website experience for women planning pregnancy, pregnant women, and relevant supporters                                     | Use for website architecture                                               |
| Provider journey                         | The audience-specific ODRISC website experience for clinicians, healthcare teams, hospitals, and institutional care partners                              | Use for website architecture                                               |
| Healthcare provider                      | A qualified professional or organization involved in healthcare delivery                                                                                  | Preferred general Provider term pending final gateway wording              |
| High-risk pregnancy                      | A pregnancy requiring increased clinical attention based on individual maternal, fetal, or pregnancy-related factors                                      | Do not use as a diagnosis without appropriate context                      |
| Preconception                            | The period before pregnancy during which health, risk, and readiness may be assessed or supported                                                         | Patient and Provider audiences                                             |
| Assisted reproduction                    | Clinical methods used to support conception, including IVF and related pathways                                                                           | Define when first used in Patient content                                  |
| Clinical workflow                        | The sequence of professional activities through which care, review, documentation, or monitoring is delivered                                             | Provider audience                                                          |
| Maternal and fetal intelligence platform | A connected system that organizes and interprets maternal, fetal, clinical, and patient-reported information across pregnancy                             | Preferred description of ODRISC                                            |
| Longitudinal intelligence                | Structured understanding derived from information collected and compared across time                                                                      | Core ODRISC product concept                                                |
| Maternal intelligence                    | Longitudinal understanding of maternal risk, health, symptoms, weight, laboratory information, and pregnancy progression                                  | Do not imply autonomous diagnosis                                          |
| Fetal growth intelligence                | Longitudinal understanding of fetal biometry, estimated fetal weight, growth trajectory, velocity, percentiles, and related clinical context              | Provider-focused capability                                                |
| Clinical decision support                | Information, calculations, prompts, or summaries intended to assist professional judgment without replacing it                                            | Use with explicit limitations                                              |
| Explainable intelligence                 | A product output that communicates the data, rules, patterns, or rationale underlying an observation                                                      | Preferred over black-box AI                                                |
| Growth velocity                          | The rate at which a fetal measurement or estimated fetal weight changes over time                                                                         | Requires appropriately spaced measurements                                 |
| Centile crossing                         | Movement across fetal-growth percentile bands over time                                                                                                   | Must be interpreted clinically                                             |
| Gestational-age anchor                   | The approved pregnancy-dating reference used to maintain a consistent gestational timeline                                                                | May use IVF, early ultrasound, or other approved sources                   |
| Patient-reported information             | Information entered or communicated by the patient, including symptoms, measurements, adherence, or observations                                          | Must be distinguished from clinician-verified information                  |
| Product status                           | The approved maturity classification of a capability, such as available, pilot, in development, or planned                                                | Required before public communication                                       |
| Cohort review                            | Provider or institutional review of information across a defined group of patients                                                                        | Provider and enterprise capability                                         |
| Audience gateway                         | The initial website decision point that directs visitors into the Patient or Healthcare Provider journey                                                  | Contains two primary choices only                                          |
| Audience journey                         | A website experience structured around the needs, knowledge, trust requirements, and actions of a defined audience                                        | Patient and Provider journeys are separate but connected                   |
| Audience switcher                        | A website control that allows a visitor to move between the Patient and Provider journeys                                                                 | Must remain accessible without signing in                                  |
| Website objective                        | A defined outcome the public website must achieve for understanding, trust, education, conversion, safety, or market readiness                            | Every page must support at least one approved objective                    |
| Primary page objective                   | The single most important outcome a page must achieve for its intended audience                                                                           | Required for every planned or published page                               |
| Primary call to action                   | The main action a page asks the visitor to take                                                                                                           | Every significant page must have one                                       |
| Secondary call to action                 | A supporting action that does not compete with the primary action                                                                                         | Limit to no more than two meaningful secondary actions                     |
| Product communication                    | The public explanation of the ODRISC platform, its capabilities, purpose, status, boundaries, and audience value                                          | Must align with Product Vision and Product Status                          |
| Product-status label                     | A visible maturity classification such as Available, Pilot, In Development, or Planned                                                                    | Must align with `PRODUCT_STATUS.md`                                        |
| Trust signal                             | Accurate information or evidence that helps a visitor evaluate ODRISC, including team credentials, methodology, privacy information, or validation status | Must not be fabricated or misleading                                       |
| Conversion                               | A meaningful visitor action that advances product use, evaluation, partnership, or contact                                                                | Not every interaction is a conversion                                      |
| Qualified enquiry                        | A Patient, Provider, hospital, institution, or partner enquiry that matches an approved ODRISC pathway                                                    | Must be routed to an identified owner                                      |
| Clinical boundary                        | A statement defining what ODRISC supports and what remains the responsibility of healthcare professionals                                                 | Must remain visible where clinically relevant                              |
| Market availability                      | The countries or regions in which a capability, service, application, or commercial pathway is approved for use                                           | Must not be inferred from future plans                                     |
| Market-specific page                     | A page containing approved availability, pricing, language, legal, contact, or product information for a particular country or region                     | Requires formal market approval                                            |
| Website governance                       | The controlled process for approving, reviewing, updating, publishing, and removing public website content and functionality                              | Includes clinical, product, legal, privacy, design, and engineering review |
| Page registry                            | A controlled record of planned and published pages, including URL, audience, objective, CTA, status, owner, and reviewer                                  | To be maintained during information architecture and implementation        |
| Canonical URL                            | The preferred authoritative URL for a public page                                                                                                         | Used to prevent duplicate search indexing                                  |
| Structured data                          | Machine-readable website metadata that describes visible page content to search engines and other systems                                                 | Must accurately reflect public content                                     |
| Core Web Vitals                          | Website performance measures relating to loading, responsiveness, and visual stability                                                                    | Used as part of website quality monitoring                                 |
| Reduced motion                           | A user preference requesting limited or removed interface animation                                                                                       | Must be respected by website motion                                        |
| Sensitive health information             | Personal information relating to pregnancy, maternal health, fetal health, medical history, symptoms, laboratory results, or clinical care                | Must not be collected through public marketing forms without approval      |
| Website story                            | The approved narrative structure through which `odrisc.com` communicates the ODRISC problem, product, value, trust, and next action                       | Governed by `docs/product/WEBSITE_STORY.md`                                |
| Every Pregnancy Has a Story              | The foundational human narrative of the ODRISC website                                                                                                    | Preserve capitalization when used as a primary headline                    |
| Fragmented pregnancy information         | Maternal, fetal, clinical, and patient-reported information that exists across disconnected records, reports, visits, or systems                          | Central website problem                                                    |
| Pregnancy story                          | The evolving maternal and fetal journey understood through information collected across time                                                              | May be used in Patient and Provider content                                |
| Narrative arc                            | The ordered progression through which a visitor moves from problem recognition to understanding, trust, and action                                        | Use in page and section planning                                           |
| Shared story                             | The common ODRISC narrative presented to both Patients and Providers                                                                                      | Adapt terminology without changing the core meaning                        |
| Patient story                            | The audience-specific narrative moving from uncertainty toward understanding and informed participation                                                   | Defined in `WEBSITE_STORY.md`                                              |
| Provider story                           | The audience-specific narrative moving from fragmentation toward longitudinal visibility and adoption confidence                                          | Defined in `WEBSITE_STORY.md`                                              |
| Clinical-value chain                     | Clinical data → Longitudinal intelligence → Earlier recognition → Better monitoring → Improved maternal and fetal outcomes                                | Do not alter when presented as the formal chain                            |
| Audience gateway                         | The entry experience through which a visitor selects the Patient or Healthcare Provider journey                                                           | One website, one brand                                                     |
| Narrative conversion                     | The visitor action that follows naturally from the completed page story                                                                                   | Must match audience and product status                                     |
| Progressive disclosure                   | Presentation of essential information first, with greater detail available when needed                                                                    | Required for clinical and technical depth                                  |
