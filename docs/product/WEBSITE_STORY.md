# 2. The Foundational Human Truth

Every pregnancy has a story.

That story begins before conception and continues through changing maternal health, fetal development, clinical observations, test results, ultrasound reports, symptoms, nutrition, physical activity, and everyday experiences.

The story does not develop only during appointments.

It evolves:

* Between consultations
* Between ultrasound scans
* Between laboratory tests
* Between recorded measurements
* Across trimesters
* Across different members of the care team
* Across the Patient and Provider experiences

Yet much of pregnancy care is still reviewed through isolated moments.

A measurement is reviewed.

A report is interpreted.

A risk factor is identified.

A symptom is described.

A scan is compared with a reference.

Each may be clinically meaningful, but its full meaning may depend on how it relates to everything that came before it.

The ODRISC story begins with this gap.

# 5. The Story ODRISC Must Not Tell

The website must not present ODRISC as:

* A replacement for a doctor
* A diagnostic authority
* An automated pregnancy-management system
* A generic pregnancy-content application
* A collection of unrelated wellness tools
* A weight-loss product
* A fetal-growth calculator
* An artificial-intelligence chatbot
* A guarantee of healthy pregnancy outcomes
* A system that can prevent every complication
* A source of emergency medical care
* A platform that transfers clinical responsibility to patients

The website must not begin with technology.

It must begin with the human and clinical reality of pregnancy evolving over time.

Technology should appear as the mechanism that enables connection, understanding, monitoring, and collaboration.

# 6. One Story, Two Audience Experiences

The ODRISC website serves two primary audiences:

1. Patients
2. Healthcare Providers

Both audiences share one central pregnancy story, but they enter that story from different perspectives.

## 6.1 Patient Perspective

The Patient is experiencing the pregnancy.

She may be trying to understand:

* What is happening
* What is changing
* What matters
* What she can do
* What she should discuss with her healthcare professional
* Whether different reports and observations are connected
* How to participate without becoming overwhelmed

The Patient story must move from uncertainty toward structured understanding and informed participation.

## 6.2 Provider Perspective

The Provider is responsible for reviewing, interpreting, monitoring, and making clinical decisions.

The Provider may be trying to understand:

* How maternal and fetal information is changing
* Whether a pattern is emerging
* Whether a measurement is isolated or part of a trajectory
* Which patients may require closer attention
* How information from different sources relates
* How technology can support the workflow without replacing judgment

The Provider story must move from fragmentation toward longitudinal visibility, clinical confidence, and practical adoption.

## 6.3 Shared Destination

Both stories converge around:

* A shared pregnancy timeline
* Better-informed conversations
* Clearer longitudinal understanding
* Appropriate professional review
* More coordinated monitoring
* Improved maternal and fetal outcomes

The user should first understand:

> ODRISC helps make the evolving story of pregnancy clearer.

The user should then select:

* I am a Patient
* I am a Healthcare Provider

The selector is not simply a navigation preference.

It is the first act of narrative personalization.

It tells the website:

* What the visitor needs to understand
* Which terminology is appropriate
* Which proof matters
* Which capabilities should be emphasized
* Which call to action should be presented
* How much clinical detail should be shown

The entry experience must not imply that the two journeys are separate companies or products.

Recommended supporting language:

# 8. The Eight-Part Master Website Story

The primary website experience should follow an eight-part story followed by a final call to action.

## Part One — Every Pregnancy Has a Story

### Narrative Purpose

Establish the human truth.

Pregnancy is not one event or one report. It is an evolving journey shaped by maternal health, fetal development, clinical care, and everyday change.

### Visitor Understanding

The visitor should think:

> This platform understands that pregnancy changes continuously.

### Primary Message

> Every Pregnancy Has a Story.

### Supporting Message

> ODRISC helps patients and healthcare professionals understand how that story is evolving over time.

### Visual Direction

Possible visual concepts include:

* A continuous pregnancy timeline
* Maternal and fetal information moving through time
* Separate data points gradually becoming connected
* A calm transition from early pregnancy to later pregnancy
* A continuous line representing the pregnancy journey

### Avoid

* Feature grids
* Dashboard screenshots as the opening image
* Alarm-based messaging
* Complication statistics without context
* Technology-first headlines

---

## Part Two — Pregnancy Information Is Fragmented

### Narrative Purpose

Define the problem.

Pregnancy information may exist across:

* Medical history
* Weight measurements
* Symptoms
* Laboratory tests
* Ultrasound scans
* Fetal biometry
* Doppler reports
* Nutrition
* Physical activity
* Patient observations
* Different healthcare professionals

These sources often remain separated.

### Visitor Understanding

The visitor should think:

> The problem is not a lack of information. The problem is that the information is disconnected.

### Primary Message

> Important pregnancy information often exists in fragments.

### Supporting Message

> A report may explain one moment. A measurement may explain one value. The wider story becomes clearer when information is connected across time.

### Visual Direction

Use an intentionally fragmented composition:

* Separate report cards
* Isolated measurements
* Disconnected clinical icons
* Independent timeline points
* Maternal and fetal data displayed in separate areas

The visual should later resolve into a connected system.

---

## Part Three — Isolated Moments Can Hide Meaningful Change

### Narrative Purpose

Explain why fragmentation matters.

The clinical meaning of a value may depend on:

* Previous values
* Rate of change
* Gestational age
* Maternal context
* Fetal context
* Relevant standards
* Whether change is sustained
* Relationships among multiple indicators

### Visitor Understanding

The visitor should think:

> A single result may not explain how the pregnancy is progressing.

### Primary Message

> Pregnancy is better understood through progression, not isolated data points.

### Supporting Message

> What matters may not be one measurement alone, but how that measurement changes, how quickly it changes, and how it relates to the wider maternal and fetal picture.

### Provider Emphasis

* Growth velocity
* Weight trajectory
* Centile movement
* Doppler progression
* Longitudinal comparison
* Maternal-fetal relationships

### Patient Emphasis

* Progress over time
* Connecting appointments
* Understanding reports
* Recognizing meaningful changes
* Preparing better questions for the care team

### Visual Direction

Show:

```text
Single point
↓
Multiple points
↓
Visible trajectory
```

The visual should demonstrate that progression creates meaning.

# 13. Founder Story Within the Website Narrative

The founder story may support trust and purpose, but it must not dominate the product explanation.

The approved founder motivation includes:

* The founder's mother experienced gestational diabetes during pregnancy
* The founder was born macrosomic
* His mother later developed type 2 diabetes
* This experience contributed to the recognition that pregnancy-related metabolic risk may have consequences extending beyond pregnancy

The founder story may be used on:

* About ODRISC
* Founder story
* Mission
* Selected Patient content
* Investor or partnership content
* Founder-led videos
* Media content

It should not be used:

* As proof of product effectiveness
* As clinical evidence
* To create fear
* To imply that every case follows the same path
* As the primary Provider-platform justification

The story should support the question:

> What if pregnancy information could be understood earlier, more continuously, and with greater clarity?

```text
Human or clinical problem
↓
```

* A verified source
* A defined population
* A relevant date
* Appropriate context

# 18. Narrative Role of Animation

Animation should reveal relationships and progression.

It should not be used simply because motion is technically possible.

## 18.1 Appropriate Animation Uses

Animation may show:

* Fragmented information connecting
* Pregnancy progression over time
* Data points forming a trajectory
* Separate maternal and fetal streams becoming synchronized
* A timeline moving through trimesters
* Patient and Provider views becoming connected
* Information transforming into longitudinal intelligence
* Progressive disclosure of clinical detail

## 18.2 Inappropriate Animation Uses

Avoid:

* Constant floating elements
* Decorative parallax without narrative purpose
* Aggressive scroll hijacking
* Motion that delays access to essential information
* Rapid medical-alert animations
* Unnecessary 3D effects
* Animation that makes clinical information harder to read

## 18.3 Animation Test

Before adding animation, ask:

> Does this motion help the visitor understand change, connection, progression, or hierarchy?

If not, the motion should not be added.

# 19. Narrative Role of Data Visualizations

Data visualization is central to the ODRISC story because the product is built around change over time.

Visualizations should help users understand:

* Direction
* Progression
* Comparison
* Velocity
* Variation
* Relationship
* Thresholds
* Uncertainty

Visualizations must not:

* Overstate accuracy
* Conceal missing information
* Treat estimates as exact values
* Use unexplained colors
* Present Patient and Provider views identically
* Create alarm without context

Patient visualizations should prioritize comprehension.

Provider visualizations should prioritize clinical review and detail on demand.

# 28. Foundational Website Story Summary

Every pregnancy has a story.

That story evolves through maternal health, fetal development, test results, ultrasound measurements, symptoms, behavior, clinical observations, and everyday change.

When these elements remain fragmented, meaningful progression may be more difficult to understand.

ODRISC is being built to connect those fragments across time.

Through one connected Patient and Provider ecosystem, ODRISC transforms clinical and patient information into structured longitudinal maternal and fetal intelligence.

That intelligence is intended to support earlier recognition, better monitoring, stronger collaboration, and improved maternal and fetal outcomes.

The website must tell this story clearly, responsibly, and consistently:

```text
Every pregnancy has a story
↓
ODRISC helps make that story visible
↓
Connected information becomes longitudinal intelligence
↓
Longitudinal intelligence supports earlier recognition
↓
Earlier recognition supports better monitoring
↓
Better monitoring strengthens collaboration
↓
Stronger collaboration supports improved maternal and fetal outcomes
```
