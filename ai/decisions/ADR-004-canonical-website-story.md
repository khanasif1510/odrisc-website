# ADR-004 — Canonical Website Story

## Status

Approved

## Date

2026-07-23

## Context

ODRISC serves Patients and healthcare providers through one website and one connected maternal and fetal intelligence platform.

Without a canonical narrative, pages, presentations, AI-generated copy, and design concepts may communicate ODRISC inconsistently as a wellness application, fetal-growth calculator, provider dashboard, or generic artificial-intelligence product.

## Decision

The ODRISC website will use one shared narrative built around the principle:

“Every Pregnancy Has a Story.”

The website will explain that pregnancy evolves continuously while pregnancy information is often reviewed in fragments.

ODRISC will be presented as the platform that connects maternal and fetal information across time and transforms it into explainable longitudinal intelligence.

Patient and Provider journeys will express different perspectives of this same story.

The approved formal clinical-value chain is:

Clinical data  
→ Longitudinal intelligence  
→ Earlier recognition  
→ Better monitoring  
→ Improved maternal and fetal outcomes

## Rationale

This narrative:

- Connects the Patient and Provider experiences
- Expresses the longitudinal product foundation
- Begins with the pregnancy-care problem rather than technology
- Supports clinical and emotional relevance
- Differentiates ODRISC from generic pregnancy applications
- Creates a reusable structure for pages, visuals, animation, and presentations
- Preserves appropriate clinical boundaries
- Supports future product and market expansion

## Consequences

- Pages must contribute to the canonical story.
- Feature lists must not replace narrative progression.
- Patient and Provider copy must remain audience-specific.
- Visual design should move from fragmentation to connection.
- Animation must demonstrate progression, relationship, or hierarchy.
- Product claims must be checked against `docs/product/PRODUCT_STATUS.md`.
- Material narrative changes require an updated decision record.

## Alternatives Considered

### Feature-Led Website

Rejected because it would present ODRISC as a collection of unrelated capabilities.

### AI-Led Website

Rejected because it would overemphasize technology and could imply unsupported autonomy.

### Separate Patient and Provider Stories

Rejected because it would weaken the unified platform identity.

### Clinical-Burden-Only Story

Rejected because it could become alarmist and would not adequately explain the Patient experience.
