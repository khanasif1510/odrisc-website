# ADR-008 — ODRISC Brand Foundation

## Status

Approved

## Date

2026-07-24

## Context

ODRISC has established its Product Vision, audiences, Website Objectives, Website Story, PRD, Information Architecture, and Page Requirements.

The next stage requires a canonical brand framework that translates the product identity and narrative into a consistent verbal and visual expression.

Without a Brand Foundation, Patient and Provider pages, presentations, generated imagery, product demonstrations, and market materials may express ODRISC inconsistently or reposition it as a generic pregnancy application, wellness brand, or AI-first product.

## Decision

`docs/design/BRAND_FOUNDATION.md` will define the canonical ODRISC brand.

ODRISC will use:

- One master brand
- One shared Patient and Provider identity
- The positioning line “The Intelligence Layer for Pregnancy Care”
- The foundational narrative “Every Pregnancy Has a Story”
- A calm, precise, human, clinical, modern, and premium personality
- White as the dominant background
- `#4d4d4d` as the primary text color
- A controlled gradient using `#ff7680` and `#80ffcc`
- High negative space
- Visual storytelling based on fragmentation becoming connection
- Audience-specific language without separate brands
- Transparent Product Status and validation expression
- Accessibility and Arabic readiness as brand requirements

## Consequences

- Website and product design must derive from the Brand Foundation.
- A detailed Design System must be created next.
- Patient and Provider designs may differ in emphasis but must remain visibly connected.
- Generic AI imagery and AI-first positioning are prohibited.
- Temporary presentation and generated assets do not become approved brand assets automatically.
- A Brand Asset Register must be maintained.
- A new logo, primary color, tagline, or sub-brand requires explicit approval and an updated decision record.

## Alternatives Considered

### Separate Patient and Provider Brands

Rejected because it would weaken the unified product identity.

### Consumer Pregnancy-Wellness Brand

Rejected because it would underrepresent the Provider, clinical intelligence, and institutional direction.

### AI-First Technology Brand

Rejected because it would overemphasize technology and could imply unsupported autonomy.

### Hospital-Software Brand

Rejected because it would underrepresent the Patient experience and human purpose.

### Presentation-Led Visual Identity

Rejected because presentation mockups are temporary expressions rather than a governed brand system.
